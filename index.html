Astro Health Check

Choose a body system, pick a condition, tick what you feel and rate how bad it is. You get a green / amber / red result showing whether you likely have the issue.

Prototype for education – not a medical device. It does not diagnose. Results only show how likely an issue is based on what you report. In an emergency, follow your onboard medical procedures and alert your crew.

The green / amber / red scoring is this team's proposal.
